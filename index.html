The trail

Five winters between the village and the far bank

One turn is one season. Each season the party searches, scouts, forages, builds, rests and travels, feeds itself, and meets whatever the cards bring: patrols, strangers, sickness, the river. The soldiers' strength and every stroke of luck come from the Fortune deck. There are no dice.

Assembling the game

  1. Print the cards double-sided on card stock, cut on the crop marks, and sort the decks with the dividers from file 13.
  2. Join the four board sheets, cut the place tiles, and fold the standees so the tabs stand as feet.
  3. Print one player aid per seat, a family board per family, a diary and a family record for the table, and the Endings booklet below.
  4. Play The First Winter once to learn the turn, then set up a game of your own from the rulebook.
